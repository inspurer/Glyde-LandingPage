[Animation frame 1: App UI selecting a fade style, head scan visualization]
1,000+ real haircuts
Every style built from real barber data — tested across different head shapes and hair types.
Syncs to your clipper
Tap to select. The data transfers to GLYDE instantly — your clipper knows exactly what to do.
[Animation frame 2: Fade-Band on head, U-ring wheel contact, glow orb pulsing, sensors active]
Sets your starting line
The Fade-Band marks where the fade begins. Same position, every time.
Posture tracking
U-ring wheel reads contact, angle sensor detects tilt. The glow orb shows whether you're on track — keep it centered as you glide upward, and your fade stays even.
Distance tracking
The wheel rolls along your head, measuring exactly how far the clipper has traveled. Precision positioning.
[Animation frame 3: blade rising smoothly, cross-section of telescopic mechanism + brushless motor]
Telescopic blade
Patented mechanism adjusts cutting length continuously — not in steps. Smooth gradient, no hard lines.
Brushless motor
Pro-grade motor used in precision machines. Quieter, more precise, longer-lasting.
